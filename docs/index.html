About

Don’t compare yourself to anyone else because we all have our own unique journey and story that we should be proud of. —

I'm a full-time faculty member in the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Sejong University in Seoul, where I've worked since 2022. Before that, I spent three years at Dongguk University.

My research is in industrial AI, IoT, and big data — I like building systems that hold up on messy, real-world data. I also teach programming, machine learning, and database courses, and enjoy helping students find their own path into the field. If you'd like to talk research or collaboration, feel free to reach out.
