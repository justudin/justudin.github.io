About

I'm a full-time faculty member in the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Sejong University in Seoul, where I've worked since 2022. Before that, I spent three years as a full-time faculty member and five years as a graduate researcher at Dongguk University.

My research is in industrial AI, IoT, and big data — I like building systems that hold up on messy, real-world data. I also teach programming, machine learning, and database courses, and enjoy helping students find their own path.
